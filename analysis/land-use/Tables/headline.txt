The Kelton sits inside American Fork's single most active new-housing corridor, so the competitive supply threat is real but overwhelmingly a TOWNHOME/attached and TOD story rather than large garden-apartment tracts. The concrete near-term threat is the FrontRunner TOD/station area (200 South, ~0.4-0.6 mi north), where dense residential is buildable BY-RIGHT and the city's Station Area Plan explicitly clusters multifamily within a half-mile - including a 15-acre investor-owned TOD parcel ~0.4 mi away. Wrapping the subject on the west and south is a ~120-acre band of development-GRADE vacant land (valued $400-470k/acre, not farm value) that is technically zoned low-density residential-agricultural but is being platted and permitted for attached product in real time: American Fork's project log shows dozens of Edgewater Townhome and Meadowbrook multifamily phases under construction on the very blocks around the subject (480-540 S / 700-1160 W). The binding risk on those large tracts is rezoning momentum, not current entitlement - and American Fork is granting it. Farther southwest, Lehi's agricultural and Planned-Community land toward Utah Lake is a longer-horizon watch (low land values, must be master-planned/rezoned). Genuinely large by-right garden-apartment sites are scarce; most by-right MF capacity is smaller TOD infill, and the biggest land banks still need a rezone. Lakeshore (Shoreline Protection / Marina), highway (UDOT), and government/HOA parcels were screened out as non-developable for apartment supply.
