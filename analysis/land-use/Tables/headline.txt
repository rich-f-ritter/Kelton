The Kelton sits inside American Fork's FrontRunner Station-Area / TOD district. Refined with the city's General Plan plus a verified entitlement/records pass, the supply picture splits cleanly by horizon. NEAR-TERM (0-3 yr): competitive attached-housing supply is ALREADY DELIVERING immediately around the subject - D.R. Horton's 'Edgewater' townhomes (sales office 351 S 1110 W, multiple phases under construction, first phase completed Dec-2025) and Woodside's 'Meadowbrook / Regency at Meadowbrook' townhomes (853 W 560 S, ~10 phases, ~half completed in 2025) - both for-SALE townhomes but city-classified 'Multifamily,' with a 123-unit 'Meadowbrook TOD Phase 4' multifamily building now under review. LONGER-TERM (3-7+ yr): the dominant future apartment threat is a ~116-acre band of vacant, largely developer-held land inside American Fork's ~444-acre TOD future-land-use area wrapped around the subject - anchored by BACH Investments LLC's 15.3-ac TOD parcel (BACH = Bach Homes of Draper, a prolific apartment builder: 350-700 units/yr, the 285-unit Bach Homes AF Apartments, the 338-unit Elevate/Lake City Row) plus Silverado Partners (~13 ac), OCAP AF TOD LLC (~6 ac), AF I-15 LLC (~7 ac) and ~71 ac of RA-zoned-but-TOD-planned family land. IMPORTANT CAVEAT: the TOD zoning that permits the city's highest density (60+ du/ac) rides on the American Fork Station Area Plan, which was still only a PROPOSED (Planning-Commission-recommended, not yet Council-adopted) General Plan Amendment as of Aug 2025 - the pipeline is real but not fully in force, and NO specific site plan or development agreement is on file for BACH's parcel. CORRECTIONS to the base analysis (development-priced land value did NOT equal apartment entitlement): Blue Spring (General Plan Residential Very-Low Density; part of its land, serial 13:041:0080, is being conveyed to UTA/UDOT for FrontRunner double-track right-of-way), the Lehi 'PC' parcels (base 3 du/ac + Environmentally-Sensitive/greenbelt, apartments only via discretionary Area Plan), and Windy City (Very-Low-Density Res-Ag) are down-ranked to Low - they are NOT near-term apartment sites. Bridges at Fox Hollow is detached single-family (87 lots), not multifamily. Net: near-term competition is townhomes-for-sale already in the ground; the large rental-apartment threat is a credible but entitlement-dependent, developer-held TOD land bank, not shovel-ready.
