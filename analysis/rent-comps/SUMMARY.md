The Kelton runs **~3 pts soft** on occupancy versus the stabilized set — consistent with rents being left on the table (or a recent operational dip), and supportive of a mark-to-market thesis once occupancy firms.

The four zero-concession comps are dominated by **Wasatch Group** assets (Parc, Rivulet, Embold) + The Vue; HelloData shows their effective = asking, so either they truly run concession-free or HD is under-capturing their concessions (flag for verification — it inflates their effective rents and PSF).
